Save the Cat!

Privacy Policy

Android game · cn.pengqi.puprescue
Effective November 17, 2026

English · 中文

Your game stays on your device

You can play without creating an account. Your board, unlocked cats, item uses, music and other settings are stored on your device. Motion sensor readings are used during play to move the planks. The game does not send your board, cat collection or motion readings to a game server.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob to show optional ads for in-game benefits and interstitial ads at the end of a failed round. Google’s User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy choice is required. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy to review or change your choice.

Google’s advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as startup or performance information. Google and its advertising partners use this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance and prevent fraud. Ad personalization depends on applicable settings and your choices. Learn more in Google’s explanation for partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your controls

You can decline an optional rewarded ad and continue without its benefit. You can manage advertising ID settings on supported versions of Android. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling deletes the game progress stored on that device; it does not delete information already processed by Google or its advertising partners. Their privacy notices describe their retention practices and controls.

Contact

For questions about this game’s privacy practices, contact the developer at contact@pengqiai.com and include “Save the Cat!” in the subject. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

隐私政策 · 游戏数据

《救救猫咪》（Save the Cat!）无需注册账号。当前棋局、已解锁的猫咪、道具次数和声音等设置保存在设备上。游戏在关卡中使用运动传感器让木板随晃动移动，不会把棋局、图鉴或传感器读数发送到游戏服务器。

广告与隐私选择

游戏使用 Google AdMob 提供可自选观看的激励广告，以及本局最终失败时的插页广告。Google 用户消息平台会检查是否需要显示隐私选项。适用时，可在游戏设置中的「Ad privacy」重新查看或更改选择。

Google 广告 SDK 可能收集和共享 IP 地址（可用于推测大致位置）、广告 ID 等设备或账号标识符、应用和广告互动，以及启动和性能等诊断信息，以投放和衡量广告、分析效果及防范欺诈。是否个性化取决于适用设置和你的选择。详见 Google 对合作应用的说明及 Google 隐私政策。

你的选择与联系渠道

可跳过自选广告，并放弃该次奖励。支持的 Android 版本允许管理广告标识符。清除应用数据或卸载将删除此设备上的游戏进度，但不会删除 Google 或其广告合作伙伴已处理的信息；其留存规则和控制选项见相应隐私政策。

如有疑问，请联系开发者 contact@pengqiai.com，并在邮件标题注明「Save the Cat!」。政策更新会在本页注明新的生效日期。
